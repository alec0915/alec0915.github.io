<h1>Welcome to my site!</h1>

My name is Alec Whyte. I'm 27 years old, I'm from Southern California, and I'm an aspiring software developer. I'm also a gamer, a craftsman, and a musician.

I studied Computer Science for three of my five years at the University of California, Irvine. I fell in love with coding my senior year of high school; my AP Computer Science teacher was incredibly engaging, and when I started to make words I typed appear on the screen or functions I made execute, I was hooked on this computer wizardry. I studied a few different areas in my tenure at UCI; I wanted to get into CS, but it was a very impacted major. I started undeclared and then moved to Math. I like numbers and calculations, so math felt right as a fallback if I couldn’t get into what I wanted. After two years waiting for an opportunity, I was finally allowed to start taking CS classes and eventually changed my major to Computer Science. I graduated in 2022 with a 3.4 GPA.

I currently work as an A/V Technicial Lead for Encore Global. I help setup ballrooms for business conferences, arenas for conventions, and outdoor venues for weddings. I'm trusted to understand and properly setup thousands of dollars worth of equipment on a daily basis. I have learned a lot about the A/V world from this job, and I started with no experience in the field. I attribute my knowledge to the helpful people who have taken the time to teach me about something they're passionate about. I like to think it helps that I pick up concepts quickly and apply what I've learned from the many, varied people I've met working this job. I have recieved a few compliments from clients and coworkers about my work or working with me, and it makes me very happy to hear I've positively impacted someone's day. It's a hard job, but the people make it a nicer place to work (especially if they like my abundance of jokes and wordplay).

In my free time, I love to game. I’m very into RPGs, shooters, and puzzle games. I don’t really like online multiplayer games besides RuneScape,; I've actally recently joined a clan and made some online friends that I play with through it. My favorite game of all time is Fallout: New Vegas; it is truly an incredible game. I do a run every once in a while to jump back into that world afresh again. I think the playstyle I had the most fun with was Unarmed because punching someone so hard it pierces their armor and knocks them to the ground is funny. It was one of the first games I got every achievement in.

I’m also big into TTRPGs. I love to play Dungeons & Dragons with my friends! I’ve been playing nearly a decade at this point. Recently, we started to play Vampire: The Masquerade, and it’s been really cool so far. I've personally run a few oneshots in 5e and a weird west system called Huckleberry. I've been planning a new one, but I've yet to put pen to paper on the concrete ideas.

I like to call myself a craftsman these days because I've been making costumes and 3d printing many varied things for more than a few years now. I've become proficient with a sewing machine, I know my printer a lot better than when I started, I've created stencils for spray painting and even designed my older brother's wedding placecards with a Cricut Maker 3. I work with EVA foam, wood, felt, vinyl, canvas, and PLA. 

I play both guitar and bass for fun. I absolutely love music. Playing along with songs I like to listen to is always my go to when I pick up either instrument. I own a Yamaha acoustic, a 7-string Sterling John Petrucci Signature electric guitar, and a Fender Standard Precision Bass.
